Mate in Three

You are White, and mate is forced in three. Black plays its stiffest defence: one road in, no way around. A new position every visit.

Tap or click one of your pieces, then tap the square to move it to. With the keyboard, use the arrow keys to move between squares, then Enter or Space to pick up a piece and again to put it down. Press U to take a move back, R to start the position again, L to be shown the solution, and N for a new position.

How to play
Moving
Tap a piece, then tap where it goes. Its legal squares are marked. Tap it again to put it down.
Keyboard
Arrow keys to move about the board, Enter to pick up and put down. U takes a move back, R starts the position again, L shows the line, N brings up a new one.
Getting it wrong
A move that lets Black wriggle out is allowed. You will be told, and you can take it back. Run out of moves and you can be shown the line.
The defence
Black picks the reply that puts mate off longest, every time. It is not guessing, and it will not help you.
Where these come from
A hundred and fifty are built in, and more are worked out in the background as you play. None of them reaches the board until a search has proved that mate in three is forced, that there is no mate in two, and that only one first move works.
